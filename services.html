Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about partnering with Zenith Recrutare, whether hiring talent or seeking your next career step.

For standard mid-level roles, our consultants present the first qualified shortlist within 5 to 7 business days. Overall time-to-hire averages 18 to 24 days, contingent on client interview rounds and candidate notice periods.

All permanent recruitment contracts include a contractual replacement guarantee of up to 90 days. If a candidate resigns or fails probation during this period, we conduct a full replacement search at zero additional cost.

Yes, 100% free. Under Romanian law (Law 156/2000 and ANOFM regulations) and international ethical standards, recruitment fees are paid exclusively by hiring employers. Candidates pay zero fees.

Headquartered in Bucharest, we recruit nationwide across Romania's major economic hubs: Cluj-Napoca, Timișoara, Iași, Brașov, Sibiu, Craiova, Constanța, and Oradea, as well as for fully remote positions.

Zenith Recrutare acts as the legal employer (handling employment contracts, medical checks, labor safety training, and payroll). The employee performs daily duties at your company premises under a commercial service agreement.
